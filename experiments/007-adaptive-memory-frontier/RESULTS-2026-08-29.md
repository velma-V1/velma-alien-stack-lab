# Experiment 007 — Observed Results (2026-08-29)

## Status

Experiment 007 produced scientifically usable paired evidence for **6 of 8 installed models**. Two 24B models were **unscorable under the run protocol** because they repeatedly exhausted the configured generation ceiling before producing a parseable answer.

The two 24B outcomes are **not evidence that those models are less capable**. They are evidence that those model/protocol combinations did not produce scorable paired data under the observed run conditions.

The earlier original 007 run is not scientific evidence: it completed its loops but produced no paired evidence because model-thinking/output handling was incorrect. The results below refer to the repaired real-Ollama run.

## What 007 established

1. **Six models produced real paired NONE/FULL/RETRIEVED evidence.** Those six datasets are usable for model ranking and downstream selection.
2. **The two 24B models did not produce paired evidence.** They must not be treated as winners or losers in the 007 comparison.
3. **Protocol compatibility matters.** A model can be operationally available yet scientifically unscorable if it does not satisfy the output contract within the run ceiling.
4. **Downstream selection must filter for scientific validity.** 008 therefore used only models with nonzero paired evidence and a valid 007 result.

The preregistered 008 selector ranked valid 007 candidates and chose the highest-ranked candidate that also passed the independent 008 live capability gate. The selected model was:

`qwen3.5:9b-q8_0`

This means `qwen3.5:9b-q8_0` is a supported local candidate for the tested architecture workload. It does **not** mean 007 proved it universally superior to every installed model, especially the two unscorable 24B models.

## Known limitations

- The repaired suite-level summary was not globally valid because 2 of 8 configured models had zero paired evidence.
- The 24B failures were generation-ceiling failures, not context-limit or hardware-limit findings.
- Failed capped generations did not preserve enough raw truncated output to diagnose whether the models were explaining, looping, or otherwise violating the terse answer contract.
- No post-hoc modification is being used to convert the two 24B outcomes into capability claims.

## Result classification

**Useful partial scientific result.** Six model datasets are valid and were sufficient for preregistered downstream model selection. The two 24B models remain unresolved.
